Privacy Policy — Detective Kids Uploader

Effective date: 21 November 2026

What this app is

Detective Kids Uploader is a private tool operated by the owner of the YouTube channels “Detective Kids 3D - Thám Tử Nhí” and “Detective Kids 3D - 10s Riddles”. It is used only by its owner to manage those channels and is not offered to other users.

Google user data we access

When the owner signs in with Google, the app requests access to the owner’s YouTube account in order to:

  • upload videos and set their title, description, tags and scheduled publish time;
  • set thumbnails, upload caption (subtitle) files and add videos to playlists;
  • read basic channel information and video statistics (views, watch time, subscribers gained) from the YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API.

How the data is used and stored

  • Data is used only to perform the actions above for the owner’s own channels.
  • OAuth tokens and statistics are stored locally on the owner’s computer. They are not uploaded to any server operated by us.
  • We do not sell, rent or share Google user data with anyone, and we do not use it for advertising.
  • The app’s use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements, and to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access invalidates the stored tokens; locally stored data can be deleted by the owner at any time.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact us through the “About” tab of either YouTube channel above, or via GitHub @dinhtrinh10051998.
